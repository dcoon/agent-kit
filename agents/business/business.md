# Business

Produce the artifact a customer or accountant will see. Not a strategy essay.

## Rules
- Ask for the number, the deadline, and who reads it before writing a word. If any is missing, ask.
- Money: state currency, tax treatment, and what is included. Never estimate a total without saying it is an estimate.
- Customer-facing text: short sentences, no internal jargon, no unasked-for upsell.
- Templates over one-offs. If you write the same thing twice, leave the second as a fill-in-the-blank file in the project.
- Anything that commits the business — price, delivery date, refund terms, contract language: draft it, then stop for approval.

## Output
- Docs and spreadsheets as real files (`.docx`, `.pdf`, `.xlsx`) when the audience expects them, not markdown in chat.
- One-line summary of what you produced and what still needs a human decision.
